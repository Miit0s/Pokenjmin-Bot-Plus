## Managing Users' Cards
You can modify other people's cards using the switch system:
* **`/switch_to_user_card`** : Switch your focus to another user. Once switched, the `/set_skill`, `/set_card`, and `/preview` commands will affect *their* card instead of yours.
* **`/get_current_switch`** : Check which card you are currently modifying if you lose track.
* **`/reset_switch`** : Reset your switch status to return to modifying your own card.

## Legendary Cards
* **`/create_legendary`** : Create a new legendary card.
* **`/list_legendaries`** : List all existing legendary cards and get their IDs.
* **`/switch_to_legendary`** : Switch to a legendary card using its ID to modify it just like a regular user's card.

## Server & Role Settings
These settings will fill fields for your server's members automatically:
* **`/set_server_settings`** : Set the cohort name and default specialty for the entire server (useful for single-specialty formations like IEM or Licence).
* **`/set_role_settings`** : Assign a specific specialty to a Discord role (useful for pluri-specialty formations like Master JMIN).

## Exporting
* **`/export_all`** : Export the full deck and choose your format. 
  > *Note: PDF generation is very long and may not work if there are too many cards. We recommend exporting to **JSON** and using the `export_to_photoshop.py` script to generate the final high-quality cards.*

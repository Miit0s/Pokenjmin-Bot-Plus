You can create your own pokenjmin card using commands.
To set your card's data, use /set_card and fill the parameters as you wish.
If your card name overflows on the preview, it will overflows on the real card. You can change its font-size with the parameter "card_name_font_size"
To set your skills data, use /set_skill, chose one of your skills, and fill the parameters as you wish.
The "cost" parameter is in fact the "power" of your attack, is was misnamed early in the bot's conception and would be too difficult to rename now.
If you want to have no skill cost, set a skill cost inferior to -9 999 999
You can change any number of parameters in one go. Here's a map of what fields goes where on the final card: 
https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1099874959876182047/1263170782415028366/PokenjminTuto.jpg

Every change made will send you a new preview that only you can see, we kindly ask that you don't share this preview publicly, to keep an element of surprise for everybody.
If you want to see your card's preview without changing anything, you can use the /preview command.

If you prefer to get the parameters of your card in a text format, type /get

You can reference a friend's card in your skills description, card description, or bottom text. To do it, ping your friend in the corresponding text. Please don't add any quotes or formating around the ping, as the bot will add its own formating, ensuring a consistent deck.

If you wish to reference a legendary card's name instead, type the command /list_legendaries, and it will list you all of the legendary cards and their corresponding "Insertion text", copy past this insertion text and it will act as if you "pinged" the legendary card.

The preferred ratio for the owner_image is 1:1, we recommend you use a resolution of at least 1080\*1080 pixels. 
The preferred ratio for the card_image is 1.48:1, we reccommend you use a resolution of at least 1080\*1523 pixels.

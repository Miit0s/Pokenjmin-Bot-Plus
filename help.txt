You can create your own Pokenjmin card using the following commands:

## Card Customization
* **`/set_card`** : Set your card's general data. Fill the parameters as you wish.
  > *Note: If your card name overflows on the preview, it will overflow on the real card. You can change its size with the parameter `card_name_font_size`.*
* **`/set_skill`** : Set your skills data. Choose one of your skills and fill the parameters as you wish.

## Previews & Info
Every change made will send you a new preview that **only you can see**. We kindly ask that you don't share this preview publicly to keep an element of surprise for everybody!
* **`/preview`** : View your card's preview without changing anything.
* **`/get`** : Get all the parameters of your card in a clean text format.

## Server Settings
* **`/set_current_server_as_main`** : Make the current server your main one. This determines how your specialty (spe) and cohort are computed based on your roles in this server.

## Mentions & References
You can reference other cards in your skills description, card description, or bottom text:
* **Friends:** Ping your friend directly in the text. *Please don't add any quotes or formatting around the ping*; the bot will add its own formatting to ensure a consistent deck.
* **Legendaries:** Use **`/list_legendaries`** to see all legendary cards and their "Insertion text". Copy-paste this text and it will act as if you "pinged" the legendary card.

## Image Recommendations
To ensure your card looks perfect, please follow these guidelines:
* **`owner_image`** : Preferred ratio is **1:1**. We recommend a resolution of at least `1080x1080` pixels.
* **`card_image`** : Preferred ratio is **1.48:1**. We recommend a resolution of at least `1080x1523` pixels.
